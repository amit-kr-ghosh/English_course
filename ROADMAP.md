# 7-Month English Roadmap

## Month 1 — Essential grammar
- Parts of speech; nouns, pronouns, adjectives, adverbs
- Subject–verb agreement
- Articles and determiners
- Tenses
- Prepositions and conjunctions
- Sentence structure
- **Practice:** topic-wise PYQs; build an error notebook.

## Month 2 — Advanced grammar
- Error detection and sentence correction
- Phrase replacement and sentence improvement
- Modifiers and parallelism
- Conditional sentences
- Active/passive voice; direct/indirect speech
- **Practice:** mixed grammar PYQs; explain why distractors are wrong.

## Month 3 — Vocabulary and verbal ability
- Contextual vocabulary; synonyms/antonyms
- Idioms, phrases and phrasal verbs
- Roots, prefixes and suffixes
- Cloze tests, fillers, word swap, match-the-columns
- **Practice:** vocabulary in original sentences; timed cloze sets.

## Month 4 — Reading comprehension
- Main idea, inference, tone and author's purpose
- Vocabulary in context; critical reading
- Para jumbles, connectors and paragraph completion
- **Practice:** at least 4 RC passages weekly; review every option.

## Month 5 — SBI PO objective English
- Prelims and Mains-level RC
- Cloze tests, error detection, fillers and phrase replacement
- Para jumbles, word usage, sentence rearrangement
- Mixed sets and timed sectional tests
- **Practice:** shift-wise/memory-based PYQs; analyse time and accuracy.

## Month 6 — RBI Grade B descriptive + SBI Mains
- Essay: structure, argument, examples and conclusion
- Precis: identify core ideas, compress accurately, use own words
- Reading comprehension and summary writing
- Paragraph coherence, grammar and formal expression
- Themes: economy, banking, finance, technology, society and public policy
- **Practice:** one or more timed writing tasks weekly; review clarity, relevance, structure and language.

## Month 7 — Consolidation and mocks
- Reattempt incorrect and marked PYQs
- Mixed topic tests and timed sectional mocks
- RC speed and accuracy
- Descriptive writing under time limits
- Vocabulary and grammar notebook revision
- Diagnose weak areas and repeat targeted sets

## Priority topics across both exams
1. Reading comprehension and inference
2. Cloze tests and contextual vocabulary
3. Error detection / sentence correction
4. Phrase replacement and fillers
5. Para jumbles / sentence rearrangement
6. Word usage, word swap and connectors
7. RBI descriptive essay, précis and comprehension

Question counts vary by shift and year. Use recent memory-based papers and official notifications as evidence, not fixed predictions.

## Weekly rhythm
- Mon: concept + topic PYQs
- Tue: PYQs + application
- Wed: timed practice
- Thu: concept + PYQs
- Fri: mixed questions from earlier topics
- Sat: RC + vocabulary + sectional practice
- Sun: revision + weekly test + error analysis
