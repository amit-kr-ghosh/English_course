# English Course — SBI PO + RBI Grade B

A 7-month, 1-hour/day English preparation tracker. The strategy prioritizes PYQs, recent exam patterns, regular revision, and mistake analysis.

## Preparation split
- 40%: PYQs and exam-level questions
- 25%: Important concepts and topic-wise practice
- 25%: Revision and error analysis
- 10%: Vocabulary and editorial reading

## Daily 60-minute routine
1. 10 min — spaced revision
2. 25 min — PYQs / exam-level questions
3. 15 min — concept learning and corrections
4. 10 min — vocabulary / editorial reading

## Daily workflow
1. Study for one hour.
2. Record the session in `logs/YYYY-MM-DD.md`.
3. Log wrong or guessed questions in `data/mistake-log.csv`.
4. Record PYQ practice in `data/pyq-tracker.csv`.
5. Schedule topic revisions for D+1, D+7, D+15 and D+30.
6. Update the dashboard weekly.

**Progress integrity:** A topic is not complete just because it was read. Mark it complete after exam-level practice, correction review, and a later revision. Progress is updated only from information the learner reports or verified study records.
